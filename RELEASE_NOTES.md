OX Link lets the OX Tone Recall add-on in LUNA reach your OX Amp Top Box.

**Install:** download the `.dmg`, drag OX Link to Applications and open it. The first time,
macOS blocks it because it isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID: open **System Settings ›
Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to the message about OX Link, and confirm. Then
allow OX Link to find devices on your local network. See the README for what OX Link does and
doesn't do.
